Our Heritage — The Aerogen Connection

Aerogen Pharma was founded to build on the aerosol drug delivery and respiratory care expertise established by Aerogen Ltd., including the clinical use of vibrating mesh nebulizer technology in acute, critical care, and neonatal respiratory support settings.1-4

For more than 25 years, Aerogen Ltd. has helped transform respiratory care through innovations that have supported the treatment of over 30 million patients across 80 countries worldwide. Headquartered in Galway, Ireland, Aerogen Ltd. is recognized as the world leader in acute care aerosol drug delivery, with a portfolio supported by more than 300 international patents and an extensive body of peer-reviewed clinical research.

At the center of this success is Aerogen Ltd.'s pioneering vibrating mesh technology, which has been evaluated across hospitals globally. From emergency departments and neonatal intensive care units to critical care environments, Aerogen Ltd. technology has enabled efficient aerosol delivery for millions of patients while helping clinicians improve respiratory outcomes.4,5

This legacy of scientific rigor, engineering excellence, and clinical collaboration created the foundation from which Aerogen Pharma was established.

Today, Aerogen Pharma is building upon that heritage by combining aerosol science with targeted pharmaceutical development. Aerogen Pharma is focused on advancing investigational drug-device combination therapies designed to address critical unmet needs in neonatal respiratory medicine, where prematurity and respiratory distress syndrome remain major contributors to neonatal morbidity and mortality.6-9

Our work is centered on developing therapies for preterm infants facing serious respiratory disease — some of the smallest and most vulnerable patients in healthcare.6-11 By leveraging experience in aerosol delivery, respiratory care, and clinical development, we are positioned to create therapies intended to improve outcomes for infants and families worldwide.3,8-11

Our heritage provides more than a technological advantage. It reflects a commitment to innovation, evidence-based development, and patient-focused outcomes. Guided by this legacy, we are working to advance the future of neonatal respiratory care and help bring new treatment possibilities to the patients who need them most.6-11
