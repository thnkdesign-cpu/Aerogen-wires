Meet Our Executive Leadership Team

Aerogen Pharma's executive leadership team is united with a singular purpose: advancing innovation in drug delivery to address life-threatening respiratory diseases in preterm babies worldwide. Guided by deep industry expertise and a forward-looking perspective, our leaders drive the development of inhaled medications while reinforcing the company's commitment to improving patient outcomes and advancing global health.

[Headshot]
Brian Noga — Chief Executive Officer

Brian Noga is a seasoned biopharmaceutical executive with more than 30 years of global experience in inhaled drug development and commercialization. Over his career, he has led multidisciplinary teams responsible for the development, regulatory approval, and launch of 10 global drug–device combination products, including Breztri Aerosphere®, Bevespi®, and Airsupra® at AstraZeneca, as well as Incruse® and Anoro® at GSK.

Brian is deeply committed to delivering differentiated therapies that improve patient outcomes by integrating innovative science, advanced technology, and high-performing teams. This patient-centric, execution-driven approach underpins his leadership of Aerogen Pharma and the advancement of the AeroFact™ program.

[Headshot]
Vidya Joshi, PhD — Chief Development Officer

Vidya Joshi, Ph.D., serves as Chief Development Officer at Aerogen Pharma, where she leads end-to-end product development across the company's respiratory and systemic pipeline. With more than 27 years of experience, she brings deep expertise in inhaled drug development, including pressurized metered-dose inhalers (pMDIs) and dry powder inhalers (DPIs), from early-stage innovation through global commercialization.

Prior to joining Aerogen Pharma, Dr. Joshi spent 16 years at AstraZeneca, where she led multiple global inhaled therapy programs from proof of concept to regulatory approval and market launch. Her leadership contributed to the successful development and approval of therapies including Breztri, Bevespi, Airsupra, and Symbicort Aerosphere across international markets.

[Headshot]
Thomas O'Riordan, MD — Chief Medical Officer

Thomas O'Riordan, M.D., serves as Chief Medical Officer at Aerogen Pharma, where he is responsible for leading the company's overall clinical development and medical strategy across all Aerogen Pharma programs in support of its mission to enhance patient care and advance industry leadership.

Dr. O'Riordan earned his MB BAO BCh and MD degrees from University College Dublin and also holds a Master of Public Health degree from the University of Washington. He is board certified in internal medicine, pulmonary disease, and critical care medicine.

[Headshot]
Shannon Strom, PhD — Senior Vice President of Regulatory Affairs

Shannon Strom, Ph.D., serves as Senior Vice President of Regulatory Affairs at Aerogen Pharma, where she leads global regulatory strategy for the company's portfolio of combination biologic products. With more than 20 years of experience, she operates at the intersection of regulatory science, clinical, and product development - guiding programs from early development through approval.

Dr. Strom is recognized for her ability to align regulatory pathways with scientific and commercial objectives, enabling efficient and successful product development. Her experience spans both U.S. and global regulatory environments, particularly in complex combination products.
