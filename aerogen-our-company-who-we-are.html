Our Company / Who We Are

Who We Are

At Aerogen Pharma, we are an innovative, purpose driven company developing novel therapies intended to address serious respiratory diseases affecting preterm infants and other critically ill patients worldwide.1-3

As a clinical-stage specialty pharmaceutical company and the pharmaceutical division of Aerogen, we combine innovative drug development with aerosol delivery technology evaluated in respiratory support settings to create therapies specifically designed for patients receiving respiratory support in acute and critical care settings.4,5

Our mission is to advance innovative therapies intended to improve care for preterm infants and the clinicians who care for them.1,2,6,7

Inspired by Innovation. Driven by Patient Need.

Respiratory diseases remain a leading cause of illness and mortality among preterm infants and critically ill patients.1-3 Yet delivering aerosolized therapies to patients receiving respiratory support remains technically complex and is influenced by respiratory support mode, circuit configuration, device type, interface, and patient factors.4-6

Aerogen Pharma was founded to address this challenge.

By combining established therapeutic compounds with Aerogen's proprietary aerosol drug delivery platform, we are developing drug-device combination products intended to optimize pulmonary drug delivery while potentially reducing the burden and complexity of treatment.4-8

Our approach has the potential to improve how respiratory therapies are administered, support less-invasive treatment approaches, and expand access to innovative treatments for some of the world's most vulnerable patients.6-9

A Commitment to Patients

Everything we do begins with patient need.

From premature infants in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) to critically ill patients requiring respiratory support, our goal is to develop therapies intended to make a meaningful difference in patient care and clinical outcomes.1-4,6-9

At Aerogen Pharma, we believe the future of respiratory medicine lies in delivering the right therapy to the right place at the right time—and we are dedicated to making that future a reality.4-8
