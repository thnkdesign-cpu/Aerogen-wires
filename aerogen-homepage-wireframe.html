Strategy

Our Approach

Our approach combines well-established therapeutic molecules with advanced aerosol delivery technology designed to deliver medication directly to the lungs of critically ill preterm infants. By targeting pulmonary delivery at the site of disease, we aim to address limitations of invasive surfactant delivery and support less invasive respiratory care.3,5,9

Technology

Our Platform

Through our integrated drug-device platform, we are working to advance respiratory therapy delivery with a focus on dosing efficiency, treatment consistency, patient comfort, and compatibility across invasive and non-invasive respiratory support systems.4-9

Clinical Evidence

Strong Proof

Small airways, low tidal volumes, rapid respiratory rates, and preferential nasal breathing make aerosol delivery especially challenging in preterm infants. Published studies report very low lung deposition for some less-optimized neonatal aerosol configurations (1 - 10%), while optimized mesh, breath-actuated, and nasal-interface approaches have been developed to increase lung delivery (30 - 50%) in preclinical, bench, and early clinical studies.3,5-8
